Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Novaswap POS payouts and security.

How does the 2.5% automated service fee work?

When a customer completes a POS payment, our smart contract automatically splits the transaction. 97.5% goes directly to your merchant wallet, and the 2.5% service fee is routed to the platform treasury transparently.

Why do I need to sign a Permit2 approval?

Permit2 allows secure, gas-optimized token transfers and approvals on Ethereum. It enables our POS terminal to execute automated split settlements smoothly without requiring manual gas approvals for every single transaction.

How do mobile wallets connect via deep links?

If you are browsing on mobile, clicking connect automatically triggers deep-linking protocols (like WalletConnect or native app schemas) to open your installed mobile wallet app securely.
